Meet the Hosts

We are Olivia and Ralph Lockey. Northside Farm has been in our family for generations. What began as a farming diversification project is now a wedding and events venue with glamping pods and cottages.

Our newest addition is a beautifully renovated farmhouse cottage sleeping up to six guests, combining rustic charm with modern comfort.

Office: 07904 119 327
Email: info@northsidefarm.co.uk

About the Glamping Pods

We have 10 glamping pods set within the orchard, each sleeping up to five guests.

  • Lighting, heating & electric points
  • Fridge, kettle & microwave
  • Fire / BBQ pits
  • Shared kitchen & washrooms
  • Child-friendly & pet-friendly
  • Designated parking

Glamping Pod Rules

  • No smoking or vaping inside pods
  • No parties or large gatherings
  • Please respect other guests
  • Dogs must be kept on leads
  • Children must be supervised
  • No access to the stream

Fire Pit Do’s & Don’ts

Do:
  • Keep water nearby
  • Supervise children and pets
  • Use approved firewood only
  • Fully extinguish fires before leaving
Don’t:
  • Use petrol or accelerants
  • Leave fires unattended
  • Burn rubbish or plastics
  • Move fire pits
